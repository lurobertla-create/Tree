About Us

Local knowledge, professional standards

Milton Keynes Tree Surgeons work across the city's grid roads, established estates and surrounding villages, so we understand the mix of mature gardens, protected trees and modern developments that make up MK — and the tree preservation orders that sometimes come with them.

Every job, large or small, is carried out to the same standard: fully insured, using modern rigging and rope-access equipment, with a strong focus on safe working practices for your family, your neighbours and our own crew. We take on residential gardens and commercial sites alike, from a single hedge trim to multi-tree removals for developers and estate managers.
